# Larkwell content strategy, 2026 (invented sample)

## Pillars
1. **Work orders that crews actually close.** Plant maintenance and field service on tablets.
2. **Billing without the backlog.** Utilities billing exceptions and meter-to-cash.
3. **Moving to S/4HANA without stopping the depot.** Migration planning for operators.
4. **Keeping SAP running.** Basis, managed services, patching.

## Rules
- One service per piece, one point per post.
- Blogs answer the search question in the first paragraph.
- LinkedIn: three short paragraphs, at most two hashtags, posted by a person.
- Case studies only for Brackenfield Waste and Osterby Utilities.

## Cadence
- A LinkedIn post every two working days.
- One blog a week, published on Monday, Wednesday or Friday after approval.
