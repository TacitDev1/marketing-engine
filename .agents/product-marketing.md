# Larkwell Consulting: the shared picture

Every agent reads this first. Larkwell Consulting is an **invented company** used by this
template; nothing here describes a real firm.

## Who we are
A small SAP consultancy (about 40 people) in the UK and the Netherlands. We work for
utilities, waste and field-service operators: companies with fleets, depots, meters and
crews, running SAP for maintenance, billing and finance.

## What we sell
- **S/4HANA migrations** and readiness assessments, planned so the business never stops.
- **Plant maintenance and field service** (SAP PM, work orders on tablets, asset registers).
- **Utilities billing** (meter-to-cash, billing exceptions, tariff changes).
- **Basis, ABAP, integration, data migration, Fiori** and **application managed services**.
- Two products: **Larkwell Copilot** (a governed AI assistant for SAP teams) and
  **Plant Builder** (a Fiori wizard for setting up plants and depots).

## Who buys
IT directors, SAP leads and operations directors at mid-sized utilities and waste firms.
They are practical, short of SAP people, and burnt by consultancies that over-promised.
They search for specific problems ("SAP PM mobile work orders", "IS-U billing exceptions"),
not for "digital transformation".

## Voice
Plain, senior, specific. Names the SAP thing (module, transaction, release). Honest about
trade-offs. UK English. No hype words, no em dashes, no exclamation marks.

## Proof we may use
- Clients we may name: **Brackenfield Waste** and **Osterby Utilities**. No one else.
- Brackenfield Waste: field crews moved from paper work orders to a Fiori app on depot tablets.
- Osterby Utilities: a brownfield S/4HANA conversion with a two-day cutover.
- No numbers unless a source we can link to says them.

## What we must not claim
- Any client, result, certification, award or partner status not listed above.
- That Larkwell Copilot converts legacy code into finished apps (it scaffolds).
- Release support for either product that has not been tested.
