Brackenfield Waste (an invented client) ran SAP PM but crews closed work orders on paper. Larkwell built a [[Fiori for field crews]] app linked to SAP PM, rolled it out depot by depot, and kept the office screens unchanged.

Outcome stated qualitatively only: work orders are closed at the asset and the register is current. Do not quote a percentage without the client's sign-off.
