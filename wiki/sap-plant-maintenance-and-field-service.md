The common failure is not configuration but adoption: crews close work orders on paper and someone keys them in days later. The asset register drifts and preventive plans stop matching reality.

What helps: a small tablet app for the three things crews do (see [[Fiori for field crews]]), notifications raised at the asset, and a monthly review of plans that were skipped.
