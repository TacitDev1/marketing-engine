Exceptions are a symptom. The usual causes are meter reads that fail validation, move-in and move-out timing, and tariff changes tested too late.

A practical order: measure exceptions by cause for four weeks, fix the top two causes in the process, then automate the rest. Adding people to the exception queue only hides the problem.
