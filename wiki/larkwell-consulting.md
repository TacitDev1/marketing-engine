Larkwell Consulting is a small SAP consultancy for utilities, waste and field-service operators. It sells [[S/4HANA migration for operators]], [[SAP plant maintenance and field service]], [[Utilities billing exceptions]], and run services such as [[SAP Basis managed service]].

Its buyers are described in [[SAP leads at mid-sized utilities]]. The two clients it may name are Brackenfield Waste and Osterby Utilities; see [[Case: Brackenfield Waste work orders]].

Products: Larkwell Copilot and Plant Builder (see [[Larkwell Copilot]]).
